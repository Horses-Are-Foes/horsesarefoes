# Business Impact Analysis

## Operational and Strategic Challenges

- Recent workforce reductions have resulted in missed business opportunities and delayed project deliveries.
- Core business processes are experiencing significant disruption, with critical items being neglected or abandoned.
- Client relationship management is compromised due to inconsistent service delivery and resource allocation.
- There appears to be a lack of accountability in project credit and recognition, with senior leadership receiving attribution for work not performed.
- Political considerations seem to be influencing client prioritization and resource allocation decisions.

## Business Implications

These issues are creating substantial risks to:
- Revenue generation and market competitiveness
- Client satisfaction and retention
- Operational efficiency and process integrity
- Organizational reputation and stakeholder confidence
- Long-term strategic objectives and growth initiatives

## Recommendations

1. Conduct a comprehensive review of current business processes and identify gaps in execution
2. Implement clear accountability frameworks for project credit and performance recognition
3. Establish objective criteria for client prioritization and resource allocation
4. Develop a recovery plan to address lost business opportunities and backlog items
5. Create transparency mechanisms for stakeholder communication regarding organizational changes
