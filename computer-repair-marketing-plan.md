# Computer Repair Company — Marketing Plan

## Positioning
- **One-line promise:** "Fast, honest, affordable computer repair — often same-day."
- **Differentiators you can actually win on:** speed, transparency (no surprise fees),
  data privacy, and a friendly expert who explains what's wrong.
- **Primary customers:** home users, remote workers, students, and small businesses
  (1–10 devices) who don't want to deal with a big-box store.
- **Secondary customers:** seniors who need patience and on-home service.

## Where Customers Find You (ranked by impact)
1. **Google Business Profile** — #1 local tool. Verified listing, photos, hours,
   posts, and a link to review. This is how people find a nearby repair shop.
2. **Reviews** — the real currency. Ask every satisfied customer for a Google review.
3. **Website** — simple, mobile-friendly: services, transparent pricing, and a
   "request a repair" form or booking link.
4. **Nextdoor & Facebook** — neighborhood reach; great for trust and referrals.
5. **Local partnerships** — print shops, computer resale stores, colleges, offices,
   senior centers (offer them a referral discount).
6. **Word of mouth** — business cards everywhere; a friend referral is cheapest ad.

## Core Messaging / Tagsline Ideas
- "Your local computer doctors."
- "Fixed fast. Priced fair."
- "We explain the fix — no jargon, no surprise bills."

## 30 / 60 / 90-Day Plan
**Days 1–30 — Foundation**
- Finalize name, logo, price list, and one-page site.
- Create + VERIFY Google Business Profile; set up Facebook + Nextdoor.
- Print business cards + a handful of flyers.
- Soft-launch: repair 5–10 devices for friends/family (discounted) to collect reviews.

**Days 31–60 — Traction**
- Post 1–2 times/week (before/after fixes, tips, customer testimonials).
- Hand out flyers to 10+ local businesses; leave cards at libraries/colleges.
- Launch a promo: "Free diagnostics week" or "10% off first repair."
- Start a simple referral incentive ("refer a friend, get $15 off").

**Days 61–90 — Grow & Systematize**
- Run a small paid ad set targeting your zip codes ($5–$15/day).
- Add recurring revenue: maintenance plans or a small-business IT retainer.
- Ask for reviews consistently; aim for 15+ Google reviews.
- Review which channels brought customers; cut what doesn't work.

## Content Ideas (keep it simple)
- Short before/after "we fixed this" photos.
- "3 signs your laptop needs help" and other tip posts.
- Customer testimonials and 5-star review screenshots.
- Seasonal promos (back-to-school, holiday tune-ups).
- "How we protect your data while repairing" (builds trust).

## Promotions That Convert
- Free diagnostics when the repair is accepted.
- Senior/student discounts.
- Small-business maintenance contracts (reliable monthly income).
- Referral rewards for repeat customers.

## Tracking (know what's working)
- Where each customer heard about you (ask on first contact).
- Google Business "calls/directions/website" actions in its analytics.
- Cost per lead from any paid ads.
- Conversion rate: inquiries → paid repairs.

## Weekly Marketing Routine
- Post 1–2 updates (review, tip, or promo).
- Reply to every review and message within one business day.
- Send 2–3 referral asks to recent happy customers.
- Log lead source + close rate in a spreadsheet.
