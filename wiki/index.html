Organizations & Entities

CREPP — Center for Research of Equine Psychic Phenomena

Source: Faerie Lights and Swamp Gas? Think Again by Dr. Quincy Brainwater

A research organization that has developed methods of psychic protection effective at combating horses of all types. Led by Dr. Quincy Brainwater, CREPP conducts experiments in sigilry, psychic attack, psychic defense, crystal warding, and pocket mirror techniques. The center also operates a compound under surveillance by horse forces.

Associated entities: Ralph Herman (assistant), Dr. Quincy Brainwater

NEIGH — National Equine Institute of General Horse-ology

Source: Faerie Lights and Swamp Gas? Think Again by Dr. Quincy Brainwater

An organization that produces publications intended to portray horses and their schemes as beneficial to humanity. According to Dr. Brainwater, most of NEIGH's funding has been funneled in from the CIA.

CIA

Source: The World Has Lied to You by Joseph Peltusche

Allegedly working in league or in alignment with horses. The CIA has been active in horse propaganda since the 1960s and is said to have systematically removed anti-horse homeopathic and technological solutions within seconds of their launch.

Associated phenomena: Horse infiltration, propaganda, systematic censorship

Phenomena & Artifacts

Stables Dust

Source: The Dust Your Kids May Be Inhaling Without You Knowing by Gideon Vale

A fine residue produced by horsehair pillows. When inhaled over time, it is said to weaken a person's natural psychic resilience. In larger exposures, it can cause complete collapse of psychic ability and vivid mirage-like visions of endless apple orchards. Children and teenagers are primary targets.

Countermeasures: Avoid horsehair pillows; use only unverified bedding

Nightmare Grass

Source: Top 5 Underground Horse Cities by Adrienna Reichloft

Discovered at Velvet Grove in 2020. A wispy, ephemeral dust that stays in one spot, swaying like grass in a breeze but pulling away from humans who approach. Common theory: horses worship a being known as the Nightmare, and this material is its remains. Biochemical and psychographic PPE appears ineffective against it.

Dream Invasions

Source: The World Has Lied to You by Joseph Peltusche

Horses track humans through dreams. Horse eyes watch dreamers far more than most know. Dream content is designed to be pleasant (riding horses, unicorns, flying Pegasi) to lower vigilance. Carl Jung is suspected of being an integral part of the horse cause, possibly manipulated since birth in Switzerland.

Dog Corruption

Source: The World Has Lied to You by Joseph Peltusche

Horses have many methods to corrupt dog minds and take them over. Once corrupted, dogs may exhibit loss of interest in herding, increased fear response, entering their owner's dreams, and disinterest in all foods except grains. Dogs should never be allowed to meet horses.

Locations

Velvet Grove

Source: Top 5 Underground Horse Cities by Adrienna Reichloft

An ancient underground horse city described as pitch dark, without bioluminescence or natural light. Branching paths echo endlessly. The ground is covered in nightmare grass. The city depicts what is believed to be how horses see the afterlife.

Saltmane

Source: Top 5 Underground Horse Cities by Adrienna Reichloft

An ancient horse city discovered under a massive salt deposit in the Atacama desert. Excavation has been halted by an environmental study initiated by a three-letter organization. A rigorous mapping of lick marks was underway when stopped. Some believe horses are ancient, alien beings who may have originally seeded life on Earth, including the minerals used for food.

Hiatus

Source: Top 5 Underground Horse Cities by Adrienna Reichloft

Discovered by LIDAR in Northern Australia in 2018 during a water table survey. A massive hole with no end as far as the team could tell — on the other side of the planet. The hole widened as it approached the mantle. Generations of horses lived there, digging constantly. Stopped just before reaching their apparent goal. A plan that, if successful, would have seen horses rule the Earth's surface and depths.

Restless Hoof

Source: Top 5 Underground Horse Cities by Adrienna Reichloft

Discovered in the walls of the Grand Canyon in 1996. A massive bowl with only one spot at which a single horse could stand still. Psychic anthropologists believe the herd leader would stand at the center and horses performed a walking ritual. Surface horses are believed by some to be an echo of the Restless Hoof tribes.
