★ God Bless You, Doctor Brainwater ★


Hello friends and fellow horse haters. My name is Kelsey Grammer. You may remember me from my hit shows Cheers, Frasier, Boss, and Frasier 2 - coming October 13th only to Paramount+!

It was three years ago today when I first met Dr. Brainwater. I was in Hawaii, working on the set of the new Lord of the Rings movie. All of the actors had been given a series of bungalows along the beach but little did I know that there was also a conference of individuals looking to learn how to defend themselves against horses.

It turns out that Doctor Brainwater was my bungalow neighbor. It proved to be a good thing because at around three in the morning, he kicked my door down after hearing me scream repeatedly about horses attacking me. I was naked and prone on the bed and he put a blanket over me and fed me a special soup that he had made. Soon, I felt well enough to rise and speak with him. At first, he was star-shocked. I have a tendency to do that to people. But soon, I realized that it was I who should be shocked.

This was the man that had been coming to me in my dreams and fighting the horses for me for years. It’s difficult to admit, but I had been tormented by horses for nearly a decade. I had been told that this was the “Hollywood Horse” by my agent, and that it was nothing to worry about.

I’ve put up with a lot of abuse in the industry, but this had been the worst by far. Doctor Brainwater showed me how to defend myself using a series of visualization techniques. Since that day, I have had fewer and fewer horse attacks and I feel like I can finally breathe and be myself again.

That night, I donated $10,000 to his foundation, which was used to establish this website. He also gave me a painting of a horse with a giant hammer coming down on it. I hope that this website is able to help you in some way that it helped me.

Unfortunately, due to a mental breakdown on set, I was removed from the Lord of the Rings movie and sent home. This actually worked out well for me because I was able to return home, where I removed all of the horse imagery and paraphernalia that I had collected over the years. My therapist has suggested that my horse statue collection was a subconscious plea for help. Having rid my home of the final ceramic horse, the phone rang. It was my agent, who had great news - my hit show Frasier was going to get a sequel.

I have not seen any horses in my dreams since taking Dr. Brainwater’s advice. If you see horses in your dreams, don’t fall for the trap. Learn to guard your mind and home from evil psychic horses who attack when we sleep.

Don’t forget to check out Frasier 2 - only on Paramount+!
