      <p>It was three years ago today when I first met Dr. Brainwater. I was in Hawaii, working on the set of the new Lord of the Rings movie. All of the actors had been given a series of bungalows along the beach but little did I know that there was also a conference of individuals looking to learn how to defend themselves against horses.</p>
      <p>It turns out that Doctor Brainwater was my bungalow neighbor. It proved to be a good thing because at around three in the morning, he kicked my door down after hearing me scream repeatedly about horses attacking me. I was naked and prone on the bed and he put a blanket over me and fed me a special soup that he had made. Soon, I felt well enough to rise and speak with him. At first, he was star-shocked. I have a tendency to do that to people. But soon, I realized that it was I who should be shocked.</p>
      <p>This was the man that had been coming to me in my dreams and fighting the horses for me for years. It’s difficult to admit, but I had been tormented by horses for nearly a decade. I had been told that this was the “Hollywood Horse” by my agent, and that it was nothing to worry about.</p>
      <p>I’ve put up with a lot of abuse in the industry, but this had been the worst by far. Doctor Brainwater showed me how to defend myself using a series of visualization techniques. Since that day, I have had fewer and fewer horse attacks and I feel like I can finally breathe and be myself again.</p>
      <p>That night, I donated $10,000 to his foundation, which was used to establish this website. He also gave me a painting of a horse with a giant hammer coming down on it. I hope that this website is able to help you in some way that it helped me.</p>
      <p>Unfortunately, due to a mental breakdown on set, I was removed from the Lord of the Rings movie and sent home. This actually worked out well for me because I was able to return home, where I removed all of the horse imagery and paraphernalia that I had collected over the years. My therapist has suggested that my horse statue collection was a subconscious plea for help. Having rid my home of the final ceramic horse, the phone rang. It was my agent, who had great news - my hit show Frasier was going to get a sequel.</p>
